- Algorithmic Scope Only: The suite strictly evaluates the geometry and bounds of vector quantization mathematically per R1 requirements and limits its scope. It does not validate real attention KV cache retention properties on actual Transformers, task degradations, nor executes long-text Llama evaluations since those are expressly out-of-scope for R1.
- No Multi-Node Orchestration Verification: Qualification executes either totally decoupled or assumes an externally managed contiguous single-GPU instance as stipulated. Cross-network execution and block-allocations scaling metrics remain untested.
- Environmental Coupling in Differential Oracles: Evaluates the original paper implementation's discrepancies through the prescribed Oracle Script, heavily depending on an exact pre-configured side-by-side CUDA 12.8 + CUDA 13.2 WSL toolkit setup without checking generic deployment compatibility.
- Fixed 1-4 Bit Limits: Adheres rigorously to the 1 through 4 total bits specified by the design paper; accuracy or limits applying greater dimensions or other precision formats natively remain unsupported.
