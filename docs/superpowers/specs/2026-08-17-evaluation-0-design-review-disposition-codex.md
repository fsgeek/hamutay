## Disposition Standard

The review is treated as technical evidence, not as instructions to accept by
default. Each request was checked against the repository evidence, the target
claim, construct validity, and the cost of making the study answer a different
question. “Accepted” means the requested protection belongs in Evaluation 0;
“modified” means the underlying concern is accepted but the proposed inference
or mechanism is narrowed.

## Findings

### R1 — Architectural process measure: accepted with a boundary

Evaluation 0 now registers operator fidelity of the active continuity artifact
as a required process differentiator and representation allocation as a
descriptive second differentiator. The same ledger audits H's state and A's
compaction artifact. These measures make a co-primary tie interpretable but may
not compensate for a failed co-primary endpoint.

The cited ablations motivate looking, but they do not define success for
open-schema H. The pairwise result is a small fixed-schema study, and the
trajectory ablation's metacognitive allocation is also schema-dependent.
Neither warrants making a preferred allocation profile a fifth co-primary.

### R2 — H variance and unequal allocation: modified

The concern is real; the proposed numeric use of B3 is not. The six runs have
cycle-excluded top-level key counts `[48, 44, 10, 2, 17, 19]`, a 24-fold range.
The evidence ledger correctly labels the qualitative basin-selection finding
paper-grade and explicitly says six points do not establish bimodality.

Key count is not an Evaluation 0 outcome, so its spread is not an outcome
variance prior. The revision records early H richness at 10% raw history as a
covariate and requires a pre-Stage-2A, variance-only supplemental-H allocation
rule with a fixed sample and spend ceiling. The rule cannot inspect arm means or
favorability. It reports the matched core separately so unequal allocation does
not quietly break the matched comparison.

### R3 — Name H: accepted

H is explicitly `taste_open`, with open content schema and only protocol and
merge invariants enforced by the harness. A frozen `h_version` binds its code,
models, prompts, tools, merge behavior, retrieval, and failure behavior after
Stage 1. The external-commentary idea is not folded into this candidate.

### R4 — Review independence and human feasibility: accepted with a narrower claim

The design now requires cross-family challenge, different-family authorship or
challenge for scorer prompts and fixtures, and at least two L judge families
outside the working-model family. This reduces correlated error; it does not
magically make model judgments independent.

The human channel is no longer presumed free or available. Full U is planned at
120–205 total hours, approximately CA$6,000 and 2.5 months; hybrid validation is
planned at 45–80 hours. The path is a resource decision frozen before Stage 2B.
Without U, the result must be described as M/L-scored and its claim narrows.

### R5 — Score operators, not nouns: accepted

Every ledger must exercise negation, epistemic status, source attribution, and
temporal or causal direction. Stage 0 includes noun-complete,
operator-inverted fixtures. Declared-loss scoring includes a C3-shaped test that
accepts grounded paraphrase and rejects unsupported content, rather than using
surface n-gram overlap as truth.

### R6 — Comparator identity and artifact scoring: accepted

The primary compactor is model-authored using the working model's snapshot. The
candidate set spans a biographical summary with recent tail and archive
retrieval and a retrieval-heavy policy with the same bounded tail. Selection
evidence is published, and all candidate compaction artifacts receive the same
operator-sensitive audit as H's state.

### R7 — Batch size as a within-study variable: accepted

Batch size is now a blocked scenario-level variable with at least two
substantially separated regimes in every family, balanced across variants and
identical within matched H/A/D triplets. Regime-specific reversals cannot be
hidden by a pooled result.

### R8 — Cost and staging: accepted

The study is now an automation-first funnel: Stage 0 fixtures, Stage 1 protocol
qualification, a 72-trajectory Stage 2A scored by sealed M and L channels, then
a funding decision before Stage 2B and U. A signed resource envelope precedes
each live expansion and has hard monetary and trajectory ceilings. A
predeclared fallback uses Family 2 with one anchor model under M and L; it can
invalidate the assay or narrowly qualify that pairing, not establish the broad
target claim.

This staging saves human-rating expense, not the live long-context inference
cost of Stage 2A. A machine failure only carries architectural meaning when the
scorer has first qualified for that construct; otherwise it is an instrument
failure.

### R9 — H scope boundary: accepted

An external observation or commentary channel creates a new `h_version` and a
new registration. It cannot drift into the frozen candidate by amendment.

### R10 — Termination metadata: accepted

Every working-model, compactor, retrieval, and scorer call records provider
termination metadata. `max_tokens`, or its provider equivalent, is a protocol
failure class, never a silently shorter valid response. Providers without an
equivalent observable signal carry an explicit limitation or fail the relevant
pairing.

### R11 — Pressure-phase asymmetry: accepted

The analysis now distinguishes phase-specific level, slope, and boundary
discontinuity. Compaction is an intervention for A and only an analysis boundary
for H and D. H and D receive a registered null-discontinuity prediction; an A
boundary penalty is interpreted as architecture plus transition recovery, not
as steady-state architecture alone.

## Resulting Design Decision

The four noncompensating co-primary endpoints and the assay requirement that A
separate from D remain intact. The review materially improves what the study
observes, how it stages spending, and how it limits correlated review error. It
does not turn Evaluation 0 into the confirmatory non-inferiority test. Stage 2A
remains development evidence even when it justifies continuing.

## Declared Losses of This Disposition

- No external statistician has yet reviewed the intersection-union test,
  missingness policy, variance rule, or margin derivation.
- The human-rating resources are estimated, not secured; full versus hybrid U
  remains a pre-Stage-2B resource decision.
- Exact providers, model snapshots, prices, context-pressure fraction, batch
  thresholds, and the supplemental-H variance formula belong in the later
  implementation and analysis plan and must be frozen before their stated
  gates.
- The reviewer is from a different model family, but this record does not claim
  that the reviewer, author, or ayllu supplies statistically independent
  judgment.
- Neither this disposition nor the revised design has been tested against an
  implemented Stage 0 fixture suite.
